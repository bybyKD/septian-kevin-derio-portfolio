The Universitas Gunadarma Informatics Lab website is the online home of the campus informatics laboratory. It is a laboratory information system and content management system, and it matters because of scale: thousands of students rely on it every single term — for schedules, news, and the KOMPRES recruitment process.

The site was rebuilt with a small team, replacing the previous system with something the lab can actually maintain and extend season after season.

## What it covers

- **Campus schedules** — the lab's class and practical schedules, kept up to date each term
- **News publishing** — a CMS flow for publishing announcements and lab news
- **KOMPRES recruitment** — managing the student recruitment cycle for the lab's internal organization
- **RBAC administration** — role-based access control so editors, admins, and lab staff each get exactly the permissions they need, with a TipTap-powered editor for content authoring

## How it's built

The stack is a classic full-stack PHP setup chosen for maintainability inside the lab: CodeIgniter 4 on MySQL, with the front end served server-side. The RBAC layer centralizes access control, and the TipTap editor gives the content team a modern writing experience embedded in the admin area.
